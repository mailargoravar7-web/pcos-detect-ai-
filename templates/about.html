About PCOS Detect AI

PCOS Detect AI is a Final Year Major Project developed to assist in the early detection of Polycystic Ovarian Syndrome (PCOS) using Artificial Intelligence and Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN).

The system analyzes ovarian ultrasound images and predicts whether the patient is likely to have PCOS or not.

This project aims to provide a fast, accurate, and user-friendly solution for supporting medical diagnosis.
